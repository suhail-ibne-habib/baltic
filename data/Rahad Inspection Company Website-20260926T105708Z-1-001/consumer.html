SERVICE

Consumer Products Inspection

Your Eyes in the Factory

On-site AQL inspections track production from the first sample through final loading, guaranteeing full compliance with your order's exact standards.

Inspection programme

The cost of an inspection is a fraction of the price of a rejected shipment. RIC inspects textiles, garments, footwear, leather, ceramics, and consumer goods at the supplier's facility—verifying quality, quantity, workmanship, and packaging before goods leave the port.

  • SA 8000 (Social Accountability Audit)
  • Factory Audit / Evaluation
  • PPC — Pre-Production Check
  • IPC — Initial Production Check
  • DUPRO — During Production Check
  • FRI — Final Random Inspection and/or PSI — Pre-Shipment Inspection
  • LS — Loading Supervision (cargo, container & vessel)

How our sampling works

Our default protocol uses Level II single sampling for normal inspection under ANSI/ASQC Z1.4 (ISO 2859-1) / US MIL-STD-105E standards, fully customizable to your requirements. We review and register your approved reference samples, material swatches, and spec sheets prior to attending the facility.

  • Samples are chosen at random by our inspector — never guided by the supplier, and never by whichever cartons are easiest to reach.
  • We and our inspectors are completely independent, and accept no favour or gift from the supplier.

Acceptable Quality Level (AQL)

  • Critical defects — AQL: 0.0
  • Major defects — AQL: 2.5
  • Minor defects — AQL: 4.0

Sampling and analysis

On request, we draw and seal samples during inspection for dispatch to you and/or to a laboratory for analysis.

Re-inspection

If an inspection fails, you may request a second one — and you pay only for the first. Subsequent inspections of the same goods are invoiced directly to the supplier, unless you instruct otherwise. The party that caused the failure carries its cost.

Loading supervision

An inspection is only as good as what actually gets loaded. Under loading supervision we confirm that the goods being shipped are the goods we inspected, and that container, cargo and vessel conditions are satisfactory at the moment of loading.

The Inspection Certificate

  • On completion of the mandated inspection, we issue an Inspection Certificate (I/C).
  • If the supplier withholds anything we need to issue the I/C, we put the facts to you for a decision without delay — we do not paper over gaps.
  • Distribution of the I/C follows your instructions.

Procedure, cost & coverage

Procedures follow ANSI, US MIL-STD-105E, SAI, IFIA, IICL, ISO and/or your specific guidelines. Our man-day rate depends on order volume and scope, and is quoted on request. Through our association with multinational service providers, the same programme is available worldwide.

  • Worldwide coverage through partner network

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where in Bangladesh does RIC provide consumer products inspection?

RIC attends across Chattogram and Khulna — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices in Chattogram and Khulna, so attendance is arranged locally rather than dispatched from the capital.

Which standards does RIC apply for consumer products inspection?

Method, procedure and standards follow ANSI / US MIL-STD-105E / SAI / IFIA / ISO, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and a surveyor is deployed to the site or vessel on instruction. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day.
