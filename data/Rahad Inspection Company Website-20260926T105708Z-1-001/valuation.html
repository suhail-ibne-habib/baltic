Property Valuation

Accurate Valuation – Confident Decisions

Independent, defensible valuations of land, buildings, machinery, vehicles and vessels — for banks, insurers, courts and cross-border authorities.

Property valuation

A valuation is only useful if the party relying on it — a bank, an underwriter, a court, a visa officer — accepts it without argument. RIC’s valuations are built for that scrutiny: independent, methodical, and reported to IVS, RICS (UK) and Appraisal Institute (USA) practice.

It would be difficult to name an industry we have not served, an asset category we have not appraised, or a valuation purpose we have not reported on. Major banks and financial institutions, multinational companies and accountancy firms rely on our work when they value land, buildings, machinery & equipment, vehicles and vessels for purposes including:

  • Lease and mortgage of property
  • Insurance underwriting
  • Tax, financial reporting, accounting and compliance requirements
  • Merger and acquisition
  • Disposal or refinancing
  • Arbitration / dispute litigation
  • Immigration / tourist / student visa purposes
  • Family Court Financial Settlement in UK, USA, Canada, Australia and other countries
  • Sale & lease back
  • Rental / lease assessments and determinations
  • Fairness opinion on an existing valuation

Our expertise

  • Fundamentals of valuation
  • Approaches and methods of valuation
  • Valuation procedure
  • Environmental factors in valuation
  • Depreciation and obsolescence
  • Financial ratios for business valuation
  • Sources for obtaining price indices for machinery manufactured in various countries
  • Technical specification of machinery used in engineering, chemical, pharmaceutical, textile, leather, garments, printing & packaging, food and other industries

Methods & procedures

Our ethics, standards, professional practice, methods and procedures follow IVS, RICS-UK and the Appraisal Institute-USA, and/or your specific guidelines.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where in Bangladesh does RIC provide property valuation?

RIC attends across Chattogram and Khulna — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices in Chattogram and Khulna, so attendance is arranged locally rather than dispatched from the capital.

Which standards does RIC apply for property valuation?

Method, procedure and standards follow IVS / RICS-UK / Appraisal Institute-USA, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and a surveyor is deployed to the site or vessel on instruction. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day.
