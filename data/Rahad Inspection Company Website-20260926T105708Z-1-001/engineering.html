SERVICE

Engineering / Industrial

Technical Integrity Verified – Quality Assured Worldwide

Shop, site and vendor inspection worldwide — technical inspection, erection supervision and quality assurance across energy, petrochemical, pharmaceutical and power projects.

Capital equipment represents a major upfront investment well before operation begins. RIC's engineers provide on-site shop and field inspections—monitoring manufacturing, cross-checking materials and machinery against specifications, and overseeing installation—ensuring non-conformities are caught and corrected before commissioning rather than after.

Typical operations

  • Pre-shipment inspections
  • Technical inspections
  • Qualitative and quantitative inspection of materials and machinery
  • Control of packing and shipping marks
  • Shop & site inspection
  • Attendance at manufacturing and quality-control operations
  • Technical advice and consultation
  • Erection supervision during the construction process
  • Visual inspection of machinery and materials
  • Independent inspection at purchase
  • Vendor audit, assessment / evaluation
  • Quality assurance services
  • Sampling, laboratory analysis & testing

Industries we serve

  • Energy and mineral resources
  • Crude oil production and refining
  • Chemical and petrochemical
  • Telecommunication and information technology
  • Pharmaceuticals
  • Power generation, transmission and distribution systems
  • Wood, pulp and paper
  • Furniture, textile and leather

Inspection method

Inspection is carried out to industry standards — ISO, ASTM, ASQ, IEEE, IEC, QAA — and/or your specific guidelines, with findings reported against the purchase contract so acceptance decisions are clear-cut.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where in Bangladesh does RIC provide engineering / industrial?

RIC attends across Chattogram and Khulna — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices in Chattogram and Khulna, so attendance is arranged locally rather than dispatched from the capital.

Which standards does RIC apply for engineering / industrial?

Method, procedure and standards follow ISO / ASTM / ASQ / IEEE / IEC / QAA, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and a surveyor is deployed to the site or vessel on instruction. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day.
