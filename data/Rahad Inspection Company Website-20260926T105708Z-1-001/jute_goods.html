SERVICE

Raw Jute & Jute Goods

Quality Verified – Quantity Confirmed – Loading Supervised

Pre-shipment inspection of raw jute and jute goods — grade, construction, moisture and bale-by-bale weight, certified before the goods leave Bangladesh.

Jute is sold on grade and weight — and both get argued about. RIC inspects and weighs at origin, bale by bale, so your contract terms are verified before shipment rather than disputed after arrival.

A. Raw jute

Quality

  • Grade, colour, class of fibre and fibre strength
  • Length of jute and texture of fibre
  • Handling, cut, etc.

Weighing

Weighed bale by bale on a platform / hanging scale.

B. Jute goods

Quality

  • Measurement (sizes: length / width)
  • Weight of bags / cloth
  • Weaving defects / scanning faults
  • Construction (porter & shot)
  • Jointing of bags
  • Moisture content

Weighing

  • Weighed bale by bale on a platform scale
  • Taring — the tare weight of selected bales is checked at random to ascertain the net weight of the bale

Write us into your letter of credit

One clause makes our certificate a condition of payment. Insert the following in your L/C:

“The shipping document must be accompanied by the Pre-Shipment Inspection certificate of Rahad Inspection Company, certifying that the goods inspected conform to the contractual specifications in respect of weight and quality.”

With that clause in place, no shipping document is negotiable without independent confirmation of weight and quality — your protection is built into the payment mechanism itself.

Inspection is carried out as per our own procedure and/or your specific guidelines.

At a glance

  • Grade, colour, class of fibre & fibre strength
  • Measurement, weaving defects & moisture content
  • Bale-by-bale weighing with random tare checks
  • Recommended L/C protection clause available

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where in Bangladesh does RIC provide raw jute & jute goods?

RIC attends across Chattogram and Khulna — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices in Chattogram and Khulna, so attendance is arranged locally rather than dispatched from the capital.

Which standards does RIC apply for raw jute & jute goods?

Method, procedure and standards follow RIC procedure / client guidelines, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and a surveyor is deployed to the site or vessel on instruction. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day.
