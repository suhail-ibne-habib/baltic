SERVICE

Fruits & Perishables Survey

Arrival Condition Verified — Damage Assessed — Losses Documented

Claims and prevention surveys on fruits and perishables — temperature, condition and data-logger evidence from reefer containers, ships and warehouses.

Overview

When refrigerated cargo arrives compromised—whether thawed, spoiled, or short on volume—determining liability requires looking beyond basic temperature readings to pinpoint whether the issue stems from cargo condition, improper stowage, or a cold chain breakdown.

RIC's specialized fruit inspectors bring over 15 years of experience managing claims and preventive surveys across reefer containers, vessels, and cold storage facilities for produce worldwide. We combine deep commodity expertise with technical and marine know-how on every assignment: evaluating temperature, assessing quality and volume, and extracting critical insights by analyzing data loggers from virtually any manufacturer—often the deciding factor in settling claims.

Exporters, importers, and marine insurers trust our findings because every report is customized to the specific cargo inspected—never relying on recycled templates. Backed by our global inspector network, this consistent level of rigor safeguards your shipment wherever it goes.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where in Bangladesh does RIC provide fruits & perishables survey?

RIC attends across Chattogram and Khulna — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices in Chattogram and Khulna, so attendance is arranged locally rather than dispatched from the capital.

Which standards does RIC apply for fruits & perishables survey?

Method, procedure and standards follow Client-specific guidelines, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and a surveyor is deployed to the site or vessel on instruction. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day.
