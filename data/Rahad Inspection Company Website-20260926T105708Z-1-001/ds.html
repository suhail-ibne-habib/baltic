Land Survey & Mapping

Precision Land Survey & Mapping Through Modern Technology

Topographical, boundary and as-built surveys with in-house 2D and 3D mapping — including digitised Mouza-map superimposition for plot-wise boundary determination.

Overview

Land project failures typically stem from two issues: discrepancies between legal records and physical boundaries, or mismatches between drawings and actual site conditions. RIC's surveyors eliminate both on-site and in-house—generating custom 2D and 3D plans, sections, profiles, and volumetric calculations tailored precisely to your project's scale and format.

Boundary surveys

A boundary survey defines property lines according to legal deeds, serving as an essential pillar of pre-construction due diligence. Our team reviews official title records, conducts physical field inspections, and overlays digitized Mouza maps onto the site plan to accurately verify plot distribution within the boundary limits.

If our survey identifies boundary encroachments, we go beyond reporting—helping negotiate settlements via easements or boundary-line agreements and submitting official survey records to municipal authorities as required.

Topographical surveys

Topographical surveys map natural and man-made site features to scale—capturing structural metrics like floor, ceiling, and window elevations, or full-terrain topography for large-scale civil engineering. Architects and engineers rely on these baseline surveys to verify site conditions before design work begins, while project teams use them to monitor quality control, track progress, and obtain official certifications.

Our land surveying capability extends below ground level and underwater, incorporating aerial and satellite remote sensing whenever project parameters require it. All deliverables—including 2D/3D plans, cross-sections, profiles, and volume calculations—are produced entirely in-house.

Construction staking & site layout surveys

Construction staking converts engineering blueprints into precise physical ground markers—establishing accurate reference points to guide structural, road, and utility placement. Proper staking ensures construction remains aligned with approved designs, preventing costly delays and budget overruns.

Engineering design surveys

Feasible construction starts with an engineering design survey requested by the lead civil engineer. Working directly with the design team, our surveyors document real-world field conditions across the development area to establish fundamental control parameters, including:

  • Adjoining street infrastructure and site access points
  • Visible surface structures, site improvements, and existing easements
  • Direct elevation surveys—topography, terrain profiles, contours, and cross-sections
  • Visible utility infrastructure (power, gas, municipal water, and drainage connection points)
  • Perimeter conditions and neighboring site developments
  • Signs of sub-surface contamination, unauthorized dumping, or illegal encroachments
  • Overall site buildability assessment

Depending on project demands, these surveys integrate land, hydrographic, and geophysical techniques—ideal for bridges, highways, drainage basin studies, stormwater management, and sewer retrofits.

As-built surveys

An as-built survey documents the actual physical condition of a property at a given point in time. Throughout construction, it checks built work against engineering drawings; upon project completion, it acts as official proof that all structural modifications match approved plans and comply with regulatory standards—built from the master base map and updated with final ground elevations and visible features.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where in Bangladesh does RIC provide land survey & mapping?

RIC attends across Chattogram and Khulna — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices in Chattogram and Khulna, so attendance is arranged locally rather than dispatched from the capital.

Which standards does RIC apply for land survey & mapping?

Method, procedure and standards follow Geospatial best practice / client guidelines, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and a surveyor is deployed to the site or vessel on instruction. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day.
