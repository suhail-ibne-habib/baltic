Property valuation

A valuation is only useful if the party relying on it — a bank, an underwriter, a court, a visa officer — accepts it without argument. RIC’s valuations are built for that scrutiny: independent, methodical, and reported to IVS, RICS (UK) and Appraisal Institute (USA) practice.

  • Lease and mortgage of property
  • Insurance underwriting
  • Tax, financial reporting and compliance
  • Merger and acquisition
  • Arbitration / dispute litigation
  • Immigration / tourist / student visa purposes
  • Family Court financial settlement (UK, USA, Canada, Australia)
  • Sale & lease back and rental assessments

Where in Bangladesh does Rahad Inspection Company attend?

RIC attends across Dhaka, Chattogram and Khulna — every major port and gateway in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices nationwide.

Which standards does RIC apply?

Method, procedure and standards follow IVS / RICS-UK / Appraisal Institute-USA, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can RIC attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day. Rahad Inspection Company has operated as an independent inspection company since 1998 and is ISO 9001:2015 certified.
