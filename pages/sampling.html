Sampling, Testing & Analytical

Representative sampling, sealed chain of custody and laboratory analysis — for any product, on a regular or occasional basis.

Service

Representative sampling, sealed chain of custody

Overview

A laboratory result is only as good as the sample behind it. RIC draws representative samples in the field — at factory, warehouse, tank or ship’s hold — seals and labels them, and dispatches them under a documented chain of custody.

At a glance

  • Any product, regular or occasional basis
  • Access to advanced laboratories in Bangladesh & worldwide
  • Sampling, sealing, dispatch & documented reporting

Where in Bangladesh does Rahad Inspection Company attend?
