Worldwide PSI & Machinery Life

Pre-shipment inspection anywhere in the world, through reciprocal arrangements with leading international inspection companies — plus longevity and age certification for used machinery and vehicles.

Service

Condition verified – machinery life certified worldwide

Pre-shipment inspection

Importing means paying for goods you haven’t seen. Pre-shipment inspection closes that gap: through longstanding reciprocal co-operation with leading international inspection companies, BCBD inspects your cargo at origin — anywhere in the world — before it ships and before you pay.

How it works

Our process

Instruction

Nomination received from the principal with contract, policy or L/C terms.

Attendance

A surveyor is deployed to the site, mill, warehouse or vessel — 24/7/365.

Evidence

Measurements, samples, photographs and findings are recorded against the instruction.

Report

A clear, defensible certificate or survey report is issued for commercial or claims use.

Where in Bangladesh does Baltic Control attend?

BCBD attends across Chattogram, Mongla, Payra, Benapole, Khulna and ICD Kamalapur — every seaport, land port and inland container depot in Bangladesh. Surveyors are based at our Dhaka head office and at branch offices nationwide.

Which standards does BCBD apply?

Method, procedure and standards follow Contract terms / regulatory requirements, and/or your own specific guidelines where a contract, policy wording or letter of credit requires it.

How quickly can BCBD attend and report?

Nominations are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Written enquiries receive a reply within one business day. Baltic Control (BD) Ltd. has operated as an independent inspection company since 1981 and is ISO 9001:2015 certified.

Ready when you are — 24/7/365

One call reaches surveyors in Dhaka, Chattogram, Khulna, Mongla, Benapole and ICD Kamalapur — and every enquiry is answered within one business day.
