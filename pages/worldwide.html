Where our network reaches

You instruct one office, receive one reporting standard, and deal with one point of contact — wherever your cargo, asset or claim happens to be.

The Americas

Cotton controlling in alliance with Transglobal Inspections LLC (Texas, USA) — a member of the International Cotton Association — plus PSI and cargo inspection through partner correspondents across North and South America.

Europe

Middle East & Africa

Cargo, commodity and marine survey attendance through recognised correspondents across the Middle East and Africa, instructed and reported through Dhaka.
