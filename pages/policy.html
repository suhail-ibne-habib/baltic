Quality Policy

Forty-five years of inspection, quality control and quality assurance — governed by one written policy, audited under ISO 9001:2015.

“Quality is our Priority.”

Those four words head every BCBD report. This is the policy that stands behind them:

  • Engaging qualified staff to perform inspection with the utmost care, skill and speed — error-free, in an independent and impartial manner, and in compliance with professional, national and international standards.
  • Working to the highest professional standards with total integrity, maintaining a consistent level of ethical work, codes and practices.
  • Applying the principles of ISO 9001:2015 wherever appropriate and flexible, in all matters of quality control and quality assurance.
  • Expanding the company’s human resources and enhancing professional skills through the training and motivation of staff members.
  • Defining quality as the result of all company activities — planned, deployed and controlled to meet or exceed the requirements defined by our clients.
  • Keeping all reports, certificates and information confidential to avoid any conflict of interest.
  • Recognising that confidence is fundamental between inspectors and their clients.
