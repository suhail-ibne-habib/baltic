Who we are

Independent eyes on the ground since 1998

When cargo transfers ownership, a financial asset is secured, or a dispute arises, you need undisputed facts. That is exactly what we have provided since 1998. Headquartered in Dhaka, Rahad Inspection Company is a leading independent survey and inspection firm. Our network includes engineers, master mariners, quality auditors, cotton controllers, and loss adjusters stationed at every major port and trade gateway across Bangladesh.

Because our findings remain unbiased and free from outside influence, our reports are trusted by international banks, government bodies, underwriters, shipping lines, and global traders. Through our reciprocal partnerships, we extend inspection services to clients across the globe.
